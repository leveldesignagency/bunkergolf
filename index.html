The system

Built like a lab. Played like a club.

Three instruments in a room built in Langen in 2025. The screen only moves after the ball has been measured.

01

Optics

Dual high-speed cameras lock the ball from impact through the first metres of flight.

02

Screen

A 4K impact surface at true scale. The picture keeps up with the strike, not the other way round.

03

Player card

Carry, spin axis, club path, and smash factor file to your card before you tee the next one.

The bays

Same data. Three ways to use the room.

Focus bay

Solo work. A full wall of numbers, drills for start line and strike, and nobody waiting on your pre-shot.

  • One player card on the screen
  • Range, wedge ladder, or a full round
  • Session saved when you rack the club

Match bay

Two to four players. Closest to the pin, stroke play, or a skins game with the scores written live.

  • Side-by-side player cards
  • Auto handicap for the night
  • A lounge seat behind the hitting line

League night

Eight players, one board. Thursday is a counted round. The screen shows the ladder between groups.

  • Season of eight counted rounds
  • Live ladder in the lounge
  • Entry included on Player and Founder
